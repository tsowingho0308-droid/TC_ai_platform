1. INVITATION TO TENDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Hong Kong Digital Transformation Bureau invites qualified IT service providers
to submit proposals for the provision of Cloud Infrastructure and Managed Services
for a period of 36 months, with an option to extend for a further 12 months.

This RFP covers migration of on-premises workloads to a hybrid cloud environment,
24/7 managed operations, security monitoring, and disaster recovery services.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SCOPE OF WORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Services Included
  - Hybrid cloud architecture design and implementation (AWS + Azure)
  - Migration of 120+ virtual machines and 45 databases
  - 24/7 NOC monitoring and incident response (SLA: P1 within 15 minutes)
  - Security Operations Center (SOC) with SIEM integration
  - Backup, disaster recovery, and business continuity (RPO 1hr, RTO 4hr)
  - Identity & access management (Azure AD / Okta integration)
  - Monthly performance reporting and quarterly service reviews
  - Dedicated account manager and technical lead on-site 2 days/week

2.2 Services Excluded
  - End-user desktop support (covered under separate helpdesk contract)
  - Software licensing procurement (client retains direct vendor relationships)
  - Physical data centre build-out or cabling works
  - Custom application development beyond migration tooling

2.3 Key Deliverables
  - Cloud migration roadmap and execution plan (Month 1)
  - Completed workload migration with zero-downtime cutover plan (Month 1-6)
  - SOC runbook, incident playbooks, and DR test reports (Month 3)
  - Monthly SLA compliance dashboard (ongoing)
  - Knowledge transfer and handover documentation (Month 36)

2.4 Key Requirements / Qualifications
  - Minimum 5 years experience in enterprise cloud managed services in APAC
  - AWS Advanced Consulting Partner AND Microsoft Azure Expert MSP status
  - ISO 27001 and ISO 20000 certifications (valid at submission date)
  - Minimum 3 references for government or regulated-industry cloud projects >HK$10M
  - Dedicated Hong Kong-based project team with Cantonese/English fluency
  - Proposed team must include: 1 Project Director, 2 Cloud Architects, 4 Engineers

2.5 Contract Duration
  - Initial term: 36 months from contract commencement
  - Optional extension: 12 months at client's discretion
