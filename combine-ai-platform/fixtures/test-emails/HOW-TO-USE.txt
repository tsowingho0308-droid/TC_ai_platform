TENDER TEST EMAIL — HOW TO USE
==============================

OPTION A: Use seeded email with PDF attachment (fastest)
-------------------------------------------------------
1. Run: npm run db:seed:test-email
2. Open http://localhost:3000/email
3. Find: [RFP] IT Cloud Infrastructure & Managed Services — Ref: HK-IT-2026-0042
4. Click "Open in Tender" — PDF attachment is passed automatically
5. On Tender page click "Analyze PDF" on RFP-2026-HK-IT-Cloud-Services.pdf

OPTION B: Upload PDF on Tender page directly
--------------------------------------------
1. Go to http://localhost:3000/tender
2. Upload: fixtures/test-emails/RFP-2026-HK-IT-Cloud-Services.pdf
3. Select template: "IT Services Tender Analysis"
4. Wait for extraction (should get 20+ fields)

OPTION C: Send real Gmail with PDF attachment
---------------------------------------------
1. Open Gmail, compose new email TO your connected account
2. Subject: [RFP] IT Cloud Infrastructure & Managed Services — Ref: HK-IT-2026-0042
3. Body: copy from email-cover-letter.txt
4. Attach: RFP-2026-HK-IT-Cloud-Services.pdf
5. Send, then in app click Sync on Email page

Note: Gmail sync now downloads and stores PDF/DOCX/TXT attachments.
After sending a real email with PDF (Option C), click Sync in Email Agent,
then Open in Tender to analyze the attachment.
